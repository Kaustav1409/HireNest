How It Works

How It Works

A simple, skill-first journey from signup to hire—for both job seekers and recruiters.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Register as a job seeker or recruiter and complete onboarding with your role and basic details.

  2. 2

    Build your profile

    Add skills, experience, and preferences—or upload a resume so HireNest can parse skills automatically.

  3. 3

    Get matched with explanations

    View ranked jobs (or candidates) with match scores, skill overlap, and clear gap breakdowns.

  4. 4

    Improve and validate skills

    Follow learning roadmaps, take quizzes, and strengthen your profile before you apply.

  5. 5

    Apply and track progress

    Submit applications, save jobs, and monitor status updates from your dashboard.

  6. 6

    Recruiters hire with confidence

    Recruiters post roles, rank applicants by fit, and move candidates through a structured pipeline.
